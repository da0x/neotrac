# Getting started

neotrac keeps a project's work as issues on boards, with every change written down,
so anyone who can read the project can see how a piece of work got done.

## Make a project

Sign in with Google or GitHub, then on the front page fill in **Create project**:

- its **address**, like `tints`, which makes its page `neotrac.org/tints`;
- its **name** and a **summary** of what it is, in Markdown;
- who sees it: **public**, read by anyone, or **private**, read by its people alone;
- whether it takes **private reports**, like a security problem, which only its people
  and whoever sent one can read;
- the workflow its first board **starts from**, which [Boards and
  workflows](/docs/boards) describes.

You maintain the project you make, and it starts with one board, named after it.

## Bring people in

In the project's **Settings**, **People** invites someone by email, with a role:

- a **maintainer** runs the project: its settings, its people and roles, its boards
  and their workflows;
- a **contributor** works in it: opens issues, comments, moves and closes them, and
  writes milestones and wiki pages.

**Roles** in the settings changes what each may do, or adds roles of your own.

## Open issues

On a board, **New issue** opens one in the stage its board starts in, with a title, a
description in Markdown, who's working on it, its milestone, a priority, labels and
files. Each project numbers its issues, so its twelfth is #12, or `tints-12` from
anywhere else.

An issue's page shows everything about it: its description, its comments, the issues
it's linked to, its log, and its history, every change with who made it and when.

## The rest of a project

- **Roadmap**: its milestones, and how far along each is. See
  [Milestones](/docs/milestones).
- **Timeline**: every change in the project, newest first.
- **Wiki**: pages its people write together in Markdown, each keeping every change.
- **Reports**: what people sent it privately, when it takes them.

On the front page, signed in, you see what's new on the boards you follow and the
issues assigned to you, and your projects are first on [Projects](/projects).
