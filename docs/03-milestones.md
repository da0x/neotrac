# Milestones

A milestone is a release, or any goal: a title, a description, and when it's due. An
issue is part of one milestone, on any of the project's boards.

## The roadmap

The project's **Roadmap** shows each milestone as a card, the soonest first, with
when it's due and its issues counted by stage. A milestone's own page lists its
issues, and says how far along it is: how many of them have reached a stage whose
status is success, of them all.

Above a board's issues, its milestones are a row to pick from, so the board shows one
milestone's issues at a time: the most recent, unless you pick another. The address
keeps your pick, so a link opens on it.

## Archiving

A milestone that's finished is **archived** by a maintainer: it leaves the boards
and the roadmap's cards, so they show today's work, and stays readable under
**Archived** at the foot of the roadmap, where **Bring back** returns it.
